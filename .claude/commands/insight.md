---
description: Write findings that survive their own arithmetic before they become sentences.
---

Write the findings.

Invoke the `insight-narrative-integrity` skill and follow it completely. Weighted means rather
than means of means, comparisons checked for whether they reverse when the groups are pooled,
unlabelled buckets kept out of rankings, and a stated base behind every percentage.

Assume the data is already clean and its coverage already disclosed; if either is untrue, say so
and stop rather than writing a sentence the numbers cannot carry.

Write it in the language the user wrote to you in, all of it, with nothing left in another.
Column names, paths and code stay exactly as they are: they are literals, not prose.

Every claim names the figure it rests on and where that figure came from. A finding that cannot
survive being asked "compared with what, out of how many" does not ship.
