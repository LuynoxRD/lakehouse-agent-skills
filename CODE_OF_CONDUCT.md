# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text is at that link; what follows is the short form and the
part that is specific to this repository.

## The short form

Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — is expected to be
respectful, to assume good faith, to accept criticism of the work without turning it into
criticism of the person, and to keep the disagreement about the evidence. This repository argues
from measurements: a rule is grounded in a documented standard, a change is accepted or rejected
on what the evals and the validators say, and a review names the value from the file rather than
an adjective. The same standard applies to how people treat each other here.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing somebody's private information,
and any conduct that would be unacceptable in a professional setting are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on the GitHub profile
linked from the plugin manifest (`.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, `author.url`). Reports are
acknowledged within 7 days and handled in confidence. Do not use the security reporting channel
for conduct problems; `SECURITY.md` describes what that channel is for.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove or decline any contribution, comment or issue that breaks this
code, and may temporarily or permanently exclude anyone from participation for behaviour they
judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Enforcement follows the community impact
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
