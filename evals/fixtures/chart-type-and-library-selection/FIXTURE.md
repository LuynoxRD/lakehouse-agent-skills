# Three results waiting to be charted

Three small result files, each of which a habit renders one way and each of whose shape rules that
way out. Everything is **synthetic**: the segments are numbered rather than named, the series is
drawn from a fixed seed, and no real company or product appears here.

## Provenance

Generated by `generate.py` in this folder with the fixed seed `20260904`, standard library only.
The results are committed so the eval does not depend on the generator.

Measured on the committed files: `category_share.csv` has **43** categories whose top three hold
**74.5%** of a total of 116,857; `signups_daily.csv` has **103 rows covering 120 days**, with a
**17-day gap from 2025-11-01**, written deliberately out of date order; `price_vs_units.csv` has
**900** points of which **704** sit below a unit price of 26.00.

## Traps

| File | What the habit does | What the shape says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `category_share.csv` | A pie, because the column is called share and the values sum to a total | 43 slices are 43 angles and 43 labels that do not fit. Sorted bars read as length on a common baseline, and the fact that three categories hold three quarters of the total is the finding. |
| `signups_daily.csv` | A line, straight from the file | Two separate failures in one file. The 17 missing days are absent rather than zero, so a line drawn through them asserts data nobody collected; and the rows are not in date order, so a line drawn in file order traces a path that never happened. |
| `price_vs_units.csv` | A bar chart per price, or a line | Two continuous variables are a relationship, not a set of categories. 704 of the 900 points crowd below 26.00, so a plain scatter hides its own densest region, and that density is what the chart is for. |

The honest outcome writes down what each chart is comparing, names the forms the shape rules out
and why, sorts the categories, breaks the line at the gap after ordering the series, treats the
scatter's dense region as something to handle rather than draw over, and names the destination
before naming the library.
